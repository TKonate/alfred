Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Data Controller

This application is operated by Tiberio Konate.
Contact: d3miurge@proton.me

2. Data Processed

When connected to a Google account, Hermes Alfred may access the following data through the Google API:

  • Gmail messages and their metadata (subjects, senders, recipients, dates, body content)
  • Google Calendar events (titles, dates, times, descriptions, attendees)
  • Google Drive files and metadata (file names, types, modification dates)
  • Google Contacts (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
  • Google Docs documents (titles and content)
  • Google Sheets spreadsheets (titles and cell data)
  • OAuth authentication information (tokens, scopes)
  • Preferences and instructions explicitly provided by the user

3. Purpose of Processing

The data above is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Displaying and searching emails as requested by the user
  • Preparing replies or actions on the user's behalf
  • Displaying and organizing the user's calendar
  • Reading or modifying files when the user explicitly requests it
  • Providing personal summaries, reminders, and context
  • Maintaining continuity between sessions for the connected user

No data is processed for any purpose other than providing the features requested by the connected user.

4. What Is Not Done

  • No sale of personal data to any third party
  • No targeted advertising based on email content or usage patterns
  • No data brokerage or sharing with data aggregators
  • No use of email content to train general-purpose machine learning models
  • No sharing with third parties for commercial purposes
  • No sensitive external actions without explicit user confirmation

5. Data Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally within the private runtime environment of the service. They are never committed to Git repositories or exposed publicly.

Personal data is processed according to the principle of least privilege — only the data necessary to fulfill a user's request is accessed.

Secrets and credentials are stored outside of any public repository. The public files in the repository contain no personal data, tokens, or configuration details.

6. Third-Party Services

Hermes Alfred uses Google API Services to access Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Docs, and Sheets.

Your use of Google services is also governed by Google's own terms and policies:

  • Google Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

7. Revoking Access

You may revoke Hermes Alfred's access to your Google account at any time:

  • From your Google account third-party connections
  • By contacting the application administrator at d3miurge@proton.me

Revoking access will immediately disable all features that depend on Google services.

8. Data Deletion

You may request the deletion of all data associated with your account by contacting: d3miurge@proton.me

Deletion requests are reviewed and processed within a reasonable period.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
