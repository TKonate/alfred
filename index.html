Hermes Alfred

A privacy-first personal life manager.

What is Hermes Alfred?

Hermes Alfred is a privacy-first personal life manager designed to help its user organize personal information, routines, planning, sport, finances, nutrition and projects with continuity and low cognitive load.

Alfred helps its user:

  • Organize tasks and priorities across days
  • Search and structure emails
  • Manage calendar events and scheduling
  • Track personal projects from idea to completion
  • Maintain continuity between work sessions
  • Reduce cognitive load through persistent context
  • Keep personal data under the user's control

Google Workspace Integration

When connected to a Google account, Hermes Alfred may request access to the following services:

  • Gmail — read, search, and draft emails on the user's behalf
  • Google Calendar — view and organize events and schedules
  • Google Drive — access files and documents when requested
  • Google Contacts — look up contact information
  • Google Docs — read and create documents
  • Google Sheets — read and update spreadsheets

These permissions are used exclusively to provide the features requested by the connected user. No data is shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Privacy

Your data is yours. Hermes Alfred does not sell, share, or use your personal information for advertising or model training. OAuth tokens are stored locally and never committed to public repositories.
